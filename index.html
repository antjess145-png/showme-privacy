ShowMe Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShowMe ("ShowMe", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the ShowMe Android app.

Contact email: antjess145@gmail.com

What ShowMe Does

ShowMe lets one Android user share their screen with another user through a private session code. A joiner can view the sharer's screen only after the sharer approves the session. Remote control and remote text input require approval and device permissions.

Information We Collect

ShowMe may process the following information to operate a screen sharing session:

  • Session information: private session code, session status, timestamps, and connection state.
  • Joiner information: the display name entered by the joiner.
  • Connection information: WebRTC signaling data, offers, answers, and ICE candidates needed to connect two devices.
  • Remote control information: approved tap, swipe, and text commands sent during an active control session.
  • Local app preferences: settings such as whether Pro preview is enabled on the device.

Screen Sharing

ShowMe uses Android's screen capture permission to share the sharer's screen with the approved joiner. Screen content is transmitted for the active session. We do not intentionally record or store the shared screen video on our servers.

Remote Control and Accessibility

ShowMe may use Android Accessibility permission on the sharer's device only for approved remote control actions, such as taps, swipes, and text input during an active session. The Accessibility service is not used to monitor your activity outside the approved session, and it is not used to collect passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal data.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, join, approve, connect, maintain, and end screen sharing sessions.
  • Send approved remote control actions between the joiner and sharer.
  • Detect whether a session is still active.
  • Provide free and Pro session behavior.
  • Improve reliability, security, and abuse prevention.

Third-Party Services

ShowMe uses third-party services that may process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Firestore: used for session signaling and temporary session state.
  • WebRTC / STUN / TURN servers: used to connect devices and route media when a direct connection is not possible.
  • Google Play services: may be used for app distribution, purchases, subscriptions, and related account/payment processing.
  • Advertising services: if ads are enabled, ad providers may process device or ad interaction information to show and measure ads.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate ShowMe, comply with law, protect users, or maintain app security.

Data Retention

Session data is intended to be temporary and used for active or recently active sessions. Some technical records may remain for a limited time for reliability, security, debugging, or service maintenance.

Your Choices

  • You can stop sharing at any time from the app or foreground sharing notification.
  • You can reject or stop remote control requests.
  • You can disable ShowMe's Accessibility permission in Android Settings.
  • You can uninstall ShowMe to remove local app preferences from your device.

Children

ShowMe is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.
